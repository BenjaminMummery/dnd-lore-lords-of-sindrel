h2. A House on Fire

_Night of the 32nd of Enean, year 4218 ([[The Soulwind Calendar | SC]])_

Greysheath was burning.

[[:marceline | Marceline]]'s body lay in the drawing room, her head removed as a trophy by Deathwalker. [[:georges-de-selve | Georges de Selve]] was dead in the stairwell, killed by his own black-powder bomb. [[:wilrin-racenglade | Wilrin]] was down. Pin and Ayr were surrounded by fire. [[:wahibre-ten-sutekh | Wahibre ten-Sutekh]] remained locked in a cupboard, and [[:harsa-nefer | Harsa Nefer]] was somewhere else amidst the smoke.

The Anathemists' contingency plan had done exactly what it was designed to do. The building was no longer a residence but a series of burning routes, collapsing opportunities, and prepared obstacles. Every moment spent fighting gave the fire another room. Every moment spent chasing an enemy risked leaving someone else behind.

The Wonch had to choose, and as usual they chose several things at once.

h2. Exit Pursuing a Bear

Learning that [[:marceline | Marceline]] was dead, [[:jean-de-dinteville | Paddington de Dinteville]] broke off his duel with [[:silrie-aegiskiir | Silrie]]. The Bear ran for the window, cleared the drop to land upon the waiting coach, and tried to ride away before the Wonch could reach him. Silrie gave chase, deploying misty step rather more elegantly than Paddington's headlong leap.

Wilrin, revived by Ayr, joined the pursuit. Wild magic crackled through his body as he hurled variously himself, fireballs, and abuse after the fleeing anathemist. Ayr bought up the rear. Paddington abandoned the coach when the flames spread too fast, his limping gait revealing the damage he'd taken during his fall. The horse, one of the few flesh and blood beasts the wonch had seen in Sindrel, broke free of its teathers and Silrie repurposed it as a war mount, seeking to run down the fleeing man.

Even in retreat the anathemist was crafty. He used the side alley next to Radwasp House as a choke point, funneling Wilrin and Silrie into one spot and detonating a container of flashpowder to blind and disorient them. Silrie lost her steed as the startled horse bolted. In the confusion, Paddington let off a crimson flare. Disoriented, wounded, and clearly expecting enemy reinforcements, the wonch gave up the chase. Paddington escaped into the city's snickleways, wounded and furious, no longer a name from an old ambush but a living enemy who knew exactly who was hunting him.

h2. The Weasel's Tunnel

Below the house, [[:arouraios | Arouraios]] fled with his document case. [[:amanira | Amanira]] and Deathwalker pursued him through a concealed passage opened beside the basement fireplace. Arouraios fought as a man who mapped exits for a living. He slipped free of [[:amanira | Amanira]]'s magic, threw his journal of maps when his pursuers would not be distracted, and tried to juke the pursuit at a junction — tossing the document case while stuffing the rest down his waistband and running the other way. [[:kithrak-ii | Kithrak II]]'s blindsight caught the trick; Deathwalker's intimidation and Toll the Dead kept pressure on him through the dark, including the psychological strike of hurling Marceline's severed head at his fleeing back.

Cornered at last, the Weasel tried the same answer Georges had used upstairs: a black-powder charge meant to take his pursuers with him. Deathwalker threw herself on top of him to contain the blast. The shared bond with [[:wilrin-racenglade | Wilrin]] distributed part of the force; Wilrin, sensing Deathwalker in mortal danger even amid the street chase, reached back through that link. Wild magic answered — a unicorn-shaped manifestation flared into being long enough to heal what the explosion would otherwise have finished. Arouraios did not survive the night. His last defiance died with him in the tunnel.

h2. What Remained in the House

Not everyone chased. [[:pin | Pin]] and [[:rossin-ros-greyhirst | Ros]] turned back into the smoke to find Wahibre and Harsa. Both were brought out alive — ordinary [[House Lithra | Lithra]] staff caught in a house the Anathemists had already written off as kindling.

h2. What the Cell Was Planning

The party recovered documents from the fleeing cell and from the routes through the house.

The book thrown to distract Amanira contained maps of [[Sindrel | Sindrel]] and sections of the [[Osteomantic Archives | Osteomantic Archives]], with route markings showing how the Anathemists had been using linked archive passages to move around the city without being seen.

A document tube, discarded to attempt to split the pursuit, held large, detailed maps of [[Dockward | Dockward]], the airship docking ring suspended above the city. Several sections had been marked in red ink.

From Arouraios's smoking corpse, the Wonch recovered a series of forged Adjutant bracelets that, when activated, showed a single image - House Mabon work passes for Dockward, convincing enough to pass a cursory inspection but no more.

These were not the papers of a group planning only to flee. They were plans, routes, access credentials, and a staging ground. The anathemists had greater plans afoot, and a more pressing threat to counter.
