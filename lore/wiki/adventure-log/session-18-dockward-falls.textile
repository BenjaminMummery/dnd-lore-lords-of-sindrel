h3. Session Overview

Paddington and Ursus argue, giving away that there are three more bombs, that the Anathemists the party killed should have taken care of setting them, and that Paddington's own agents will now move on to setting the remaining bombs. Amanira has a map of the bombing sites, so they know where to go.

The party activate an ancient portal gate, getting them to the Airport.

They find and disarm the bomb, as well as learning that the rude people who set it were heading next to the Prow.

At the prow, an anathemist rearguard provides a complication. They disarm the second bomb, but learn that Galoras has been kidnapped by the saboteurs. There's no time to make it to the final location on foot, Mabon leadership lends them The Audacity - an experimental interceptor class airship.

Finally at the warehouse they show down the remaining anathemists. 

In their moment of triumph, Galoras reveals themselves to be a traitor and activates Ayr's sword. The party have a tiny window of time to contain a highly dangerous magical explosion.
