h2. The House Wakes

_Night of the 32nd of Enean, year 4218 ([[The Soulwind Calendar | SC]])_

The Wonch had divided Greysheath between them, but the house was not as unaware of their presence as they had hoped. A disturbance on the upper floors drew the household's attention, and careful infiltration became confrontation almost at once.

The Anathemists did not react like frightened servants or cornered smugglers. They reacted like people who had prepared the building to become a weapon. Doors, passages, obstacles, smoke, fire, and hidden routes all served the same plan. Every delay bought time to burn evidence. Every sealed shutter turned a respectable townhouse into a kiln. Physical attacks were turned aside by armour concealed under their civilian clothing.

Their hatred of magic was just as obvious as their preparation. The cell did not merely oppose the Wonch as investigators, they singled out anyone who cast a spell, treated arcane working as moral contamination, and answered every display of magic with particular contempt and violence. To the Anathemists, the party's powers were proof of the evil they had come to eradicate.

h2. Goldfinger

[[:regnif-d-log | Regnif d'Log]] appeared in the confusion, strangely out of place in his silk dressing gown among the battle-equipped combatants. The name from the gate records was only a cover; the man was Baron Goldfinger, a spectre from [[:ayr | Ayr's]] past. He seemed as surprised to see the gnome as Ayr was to see him.

The reunion was short lived. Apparently concluding that the Wonch were there at Ayr's behest, he began attempting to sway them. Ayr, however, had no interest in indulging the villainous noble's honeyed words. Goldfinger was knocked to the floor unconscious in the scuffle.

However far Ayr had intended his vengeance to go, the insidious voice of the shield on his wrist urged him to go further. Whatever restraint remained was not enough. Galoras's patient engineering had been storing up power in the Journeyman's Greeting for several days, and Ayr unleashed all of it in one furious blow that clove not just Goldfinger but the floor beneath. Pin, on the floor below, was nearly caught in the blade's deadly arc. Goldfinger was dead before the Wonch could question him. They had confirmation of the noble's identity, but not the shape of the network beyond Greysheath's walls.

h2. The Burn Plan

The Anathemists moved at once to the next stage. Prepared traps fired throughout the house. Incendiaries spread fire along routes the Wonch had just opened. Smoke filled the passages. Servants' exits became hazards as doors sealed, obstructions fell, and the building's carefully arranged locks opened and closed on a plan the party had not yet read.

[[:arouraios | Arouraios]] seized a case of documents and made for the basement. [[:amanira | Amanira]] pursued him, while [[:kithrak-ii | Kithrak II]], Deathwalker, and [[:tama | Tama]] fought through the upper chaos. The Weasel was no longer preserving cover. He was trying to get the intelligence out.

[[:marceline | Marceline]] was caught near a window. The Snake used smoke and flame as cover, fighting to escape rather than to stand and die — but the Wonch held her in place. When her last routes failed, she was killed. The party later removed her head, leaving no doubt that Marceline would not coordinate another operation from Ambassador Street.

h2. The Bears Arrive

The fire and fighting brought [[:georges-de-selve | Georges de Selve]] and [[:jean-de-dinteville | Paddington de Dinteville]] into the house.

Georges fought [[:pin | Pin]], Wilrin, and Ayr in the stairwell. He was losing, and the outcome was becoming clear. Rather than surrender, he detonated a black-powder charge in an attempt to take his opponents with him. The explosion killed Georges, knocked Wilrin down in the entrance hall, and set the stairwell burning around Pin and Ayr. It also bought the surviving agents the confusion they needed to keep moving.

Upstairs, [[:silrie-aegiskiir | Silrie]] fought [[:jean-de-dinteville | Paddington de Dinteville]] — the dangerous merchant from the White Dragon road, now stripped of his travelling alias. He fought as though the household could still be saved if enough people died quickly enough.

h2. The House in Flames

Mere minutes after the infiltration began, Greysheath had become several battles at once. Arouraios had escaped toward the basement with the document case, pursued by Amanira, Kithrak, and Deathwalker. Silrie remained engaged with Paddington. Wilrin lay unconscious in the entrance hall. Pin and Ayr were trapped in a burning stairwell with Georges dead at their feet. [[:wahibre-ten-sutekh | Wahibre ten-Sutekh]] was locked in a cupboard; [[:harsa-nefer | Harsa Nefer]] was somewhere else in the smoke — both in severe danger of burning to death.

The Wonch had found the Anathemists' house. They had also found the cost of entering it.
