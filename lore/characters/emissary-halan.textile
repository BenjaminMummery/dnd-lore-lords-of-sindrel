You see a young dwarf woman of medium build — compact and deliberate, with the upright carriage of someone who has spent years being looked at across negotiation tables. Her skin is warm bronze-brown, smooth over strong cheekbones and a rounded jaw; her eyes are dark and attentive, kohl-dark at the lash line in the Lithran manner, so her gaze reads as framed rather than bare. Her dark hair is dressed into an elegant halo of small decorative curls that crown her head without hiding it; her beard is dark, full enough to mark her as dwarven, but trimmed close and shaped to echo the curve of her jaw — groomed as carefully as any [[Factor | Factor]]'s collar.

She is turned out in full [[House Lithra | Lithra]] formal dress for [[Sindrel | Sindrel]]'s indoor season: layered ash-coloured silk and fine smoke-grey wool, cut in long vertical lines with pleated panels that fall cleanly from shoulder to hem. Over this she wears a pleated silver mantle of gauze-thin drapery — sheer enough to suggest bare shoulders beneath without quite showing them — and the house's monochrome palette runs through every layer: pearl, slate, pewter, charcoal, with only the faintest thread of muted gold at a cuff or hem. At her throat sits a distinctive usekh — a beaded crescent gorget of silver and pale stone that frames her face and beard, drawing the eye to her mouth before anything else. A silver eye-clasp at one shoulder pinches the mantle in place — the Lithra symbol worn openly, but as warning rather than boast. Her hands are gloved in grey doeskin thin enough for coded gestures; a signet ring on one finger bears a geometric border in the Egyptian-influenced Lithran style.

She holds herself with composed poise — chin level, shoulders easy, every fold arranged so she seems elegant, expensive, and difficult to accuse of vanity. She speaks with the hint of a smile in her voice, always slightly amused, slightly charmed, giving every outward sign of enjoying the conversation.

[[Factor | Factor]] of [[House Lithra | House Lithra]].

* *28th of Enean, year 4218 ([[The Soulwind Calendar | SC]])*[1]
** [[:ayr | Ayr]], disguised as "Fenton Woofrunner", met Halan at a Lithran soiree during the day's downtime.
* *29th of Enean, year 4218 ([[The Soulwind Calendar | SC]])*[2]
** Convened the [[Hexine Circle | Hexine Circle]] in the morning after [[:hielaman | Captain Hielaman]]'s interrogation exposed a leak; introduced the house representatives and tasked [[The Adventurers | the Wonch]] with finding spies in [[Sindrel | Sindrel]].
** Agreed to help [[:silrie-aegiskiir | Silrie]] locate blueprints of the city's oldest structures during the house interviews that followed immediately (each house keeps its own records).

h2. Spies lead:

bq). "The only true power is information, and the key to information is access. They can only have learned what they know from highly placed individuals - 20 or so in the entire city I suspect. If you want to get access to those sorts of social circles, you need to seem like you belong. I'd imaging you could get into one of my soirees with a wink and a suitably daring fake name, but the upper circles are a different story. You would need money, and lots of it. Enough for an opulent lifestyle  and even more for bribes and other social lubrication. So too you would need to appear to be socially influential and reliably stable - old money gentry, or hereditary nobility, but with a legend that is unlikely to contradict anyone's experiences. They couldn't risk changing alias within the city, they'll have arrived in character. I would suggest looking at recent arrivals, within the last few months. Focus on the nobility and wealthier merchants - people who can move large amounts of money without arousing suspicion. Finally look for people who are obviously harmless, someone around whom it would be easy to drop your guard. For example, a young naive noblewoman with a disarmingly eager affect travelling with a fortune in gold and an almost comically proficient bodyguard...
<br>
I jest of course, I have confirmed the Lady Von Pthasia's identity myself, and either way she only arrived in the city _after_ the information loss had occurred. Although, I must confess may be inclined to use certain details of her life the next time I myself have to go undercover."
