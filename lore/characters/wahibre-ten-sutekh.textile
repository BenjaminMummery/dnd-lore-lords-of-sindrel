You see a dwarf in his thirties with a warm, weathered complexion and a compact, work-hardened build. His beard is trimmed close; his hair is kept practical under a Lithra-grey cap when he wears one. He is turned out as a [[House Lithra | Lithra]] household [[Adjutant | adjutant]] attached to a diplomatic residence: charcoal-grey wool livery, scuffed boots, and a gold adjutant bracelet at the wrist — house patronage, not a visitor's silver guest band. He presents as polite and visibly nervous, the sort of servant who fills frightened silence with too many words. He speaks quickly when alarmed, then remembers himself and tries to bow his way back to propriety.

[[Adjutant | Adjutant]] of [[House Lithra | House Lithra]] — scullery and back-stair staff at Greysheath House on Ambassador Street, opposite [[Radwasp House | Radwasp House]].

h2. Prep

* **Duties:** scullery, coal scuttles to the cellar hatch, boots and mud at the back stair, hauling trays when told. Genuine household staff — not Anathemist cover.
* Greysheath's charcoal volume far exceeds heating need; Wahibre executes deliveries without understanding the cell's gunpowder logistics.
* If questioned after the fire: frightened, talkative, no clear picture of Marceline's orders beyond "master wanted the house warm."
