You see a human woman in her forties with warm brown skin and a slight, tired build — the posture of someone who has been on her feet since before dawn. Her grey hair is coiled at the nape in a style meant to stay neat through a long shift. She wears [[House Lithra | Lithra]] household grey: layered ash wool and linen, practical rather than theatrical, with a gold adjutant bracelet at the wrist. She presents as quiet and professionally invisible, the kind of upper-floor servant guests forget until they need fresh linens. She speaks softly, in short sentences, and does not volunteer what she was not asked.

<!-- OP_BIO -->
[[Adjutant | Adjutant]] of [[House Lithra | House Lithra]] — upstairs and pantry staff at Greysheath House on Ambassador Street.

* *32nd of Enean, year 4218 ([[The Soulwind Calendar | SC]])*[1]
** Named by [[:cedric | Cedric]] at [[Radwasp House | Radwasp House]] as one of Greysheath's Lithra adjutants, alongside [[:wahibre-ten-sutekh | Wahibre ten-Sutekh]]; he saw no suspicious behaviour from either, though charcoal deliveries to the house had been unusually frequent.[2]

h2. Prep

* **Duties:** upstairs rooms, linens, fires, plate; keeps kitchen and pantry vaguely in order. Genuine household staff — not Anathemist cover.
* **Household calendar (investigation clue):** Harsa records linen days, formal dinners, and nights when [[:marceline | Marceline]] or [[:arouraios | Arouraios]] told staff not to wait up. Unexplained absences align with city-wide sabotage dates (Grimbolg flood, [[:nielsenn | Nielsenn]]'s disappearance, Beltus summoning, Isius scandal, later Goela / Mabon / Meness incidents). Recoverable from Greysheath if the party searches before or despite the fire — otherwise GM discretion whether pages survived.
* After rescue: exhausted, loyal to procedure, unlikely to lie for the cell; may not have understood what she was recording.
