You see a human woman in her thirties with deep brown skin and a slender, upright build — tall enough to carry height without effort, and dressed so that every line of her body reads vertical and deliberate. Her head is shaved smooth, the scalp oiled to a soft gleam that catches lamplight; no facial hair. A small mephit perches on her right shoulder, wings folded and eyes bright — the familiar mark of a [[House Beltus | Beltus]] summoner who expects you to notice the retinue before you notice the person.

She wears [[House Beltus | Beltus]] formal dress built for spectacle and implied servants: a long black silk robe over a narrow fitted underlayer, the outer fabric falling in dramatic vertical folds weighted at the hem so it trails just enough to demand space. Black and gold dominate — arabesque gold embroidery runs along collar, cuffs, and the front panel in dense geometric borders; gilt chains loop from shoulder to sash-belt, and jewelled cuffs of amber and onyx click softly when she moves. Sheer smoke-coloured veils drape from her shoulders, layered over the black rather than concealing it, and a high starched collar rises behind her neck like a frame. The garment is the sort that implies an entire team of unseen hands helping her through every doorway — which, for a [[Factor | Factor]] of Beltus, is precisely the point. She presents as effortlessly confident and personable, though irritation shows quickly when bureaucracy wastes her time. Her voice stays soft and even, yet carries an authority that expects to be heard without repetition.
