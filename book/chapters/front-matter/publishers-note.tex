This edition has been prepared for travellers, merchants, and registered guests who require a reliable account of Arcan and its northern realms. The compilers have drawn upon house records, caravan reports, and scholarly correspondence wherever primary sources permitted.

Where accounts conflict, we have favoured testimony corroborated by more than one reputable witness. The reader is advised that distant courts may hold opinions at variance with those printed here.
