\section{Scope and Arrangement}

This guide proceeds from the widest lens to the narrowest. Part~I treats Arcan as a whole. Part~II treats the northern continent of Artresh. Later parts apply the same structure to individual realms---Soulwind, Fallowhurst, Tellarin, the lands of old Houd, and finally Sindrel---before the reference appendices.
